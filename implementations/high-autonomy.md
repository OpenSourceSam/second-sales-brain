## The Fully Integrated Workflow

This is the most powerful setup:

1. **Notion database** with the full SSB schema
2. **Claude Project** with custom instructions understanding the SSB
3. **MCP integration** — Claude reads and writes to Notion directly
4. **Capture:** Paste an email into Claude → auto-scores, auto-tags, asks for corrections → writes entry to Notion
5. **Retrieve:** "I'm writing to a VP of Engineering at a healthcare company. Show me my top 3 emails to similar personas."
6. **Review:** "Analyze my last 30 emails. What distinguishes Gold from Bad? What tags am I underusing?"
7. **Draft:** Claude writes informed by your entire SSB — your voice, your patterns, your insights

---

## What Makes This Different From Just Using Claude

Without the SSB, Claude writes generic emails. With the SSB, Claude writes emails informed by YOUR history of what actually worked for YOUR prospects in YOUR industry. The SSB is the training data that makes AI assistance actually personalized.

## Beyond Email: Additional SSB Modules

The email library is the foundation. These modules extend the SSB using the Claude + Notion stack. See the [main README](../README.md) for the complete framework.

### Account & Vertical Research

- **Claude Projects with MCP** -- AI reads your Notion research database directly, no copy-paste
- Create **linked Notion databases:** Accounts, Verticals, Contacts, all related to your SSB
- Claude performs deep research and **writes findings back to Notion automatically**
- Pre-call briefs generated from your account database + live web research
- Vertical intelligence compounds -- each account you study makes the next one in that industry richer

### Automation & List Building

- **MCP integration:** Claude reads ICP criteria from Notion, builds prospect lists, writes them back
- **Zapier/Make:** auto-forward sent emails -> Claude API -> tag and write to Notion
- Scheduled **Claude sessions** can batch-process new prospects overnight
- **Notion views** act as dynamic filters: hot prospects, stale leads, untouched verticals
- Full read/write means zero manual data entry once configured

### Cold Calling Support

- **Pre-call:** Claude pulls account history, recent outreach, and research from Notion in one query
- **Real-time objection prep:** "What objections has [persona type] raised in my past conversations?"
- **Post-call:** dictate notes -> Claude tags, scores, and updates the account record in Notion
- **Call scripts** built from your Notion SSB data -- personalized to the prospect, not templated
- **MCP gives Claude access to your full history** during the conversation, not just what you paste in
